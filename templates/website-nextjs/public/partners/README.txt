Partner logos.

Drop a file here named after the partner slug, e.g. braveworks.png, then point
the row at it:

  set_partner_logo  slug=braveworks  logo_url=/partners/braveworks.png

(or set partners.logo_url directly in Supabase).

PNG or SVG with a transparent background is ideal, but a white ground is fine —
the card sits every logo in a white plate so it looks deliberate either way.
Roughly 400px on the long edge is plenty; the card renders at 30px tall.

Use the logo the organization gives you. Nothing here should be scraped.
